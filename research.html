Research

A selection of past and present research endeavors.

Building a Visual Analytic System for Individuals Living with Parkinson's Disease

Advisor: Danielle Albers Szafir, Mentor: Jade Kandel

VisuaLab at UNC Chapel Hill

I participated in developing an interactive system to monitor the daily activities of individuals living with Parkinson's disease, integrating augmented reality technology and immersive analytics components.

Tracking Climate Change with Satellites and Artificial Intelligence

Advisor: Kyle Bradbury

Duke Bass Connections Team

I am currently leading team efforts to analyze satellite imagery and other remotely sensed data using computer vision techniques to better understand and plan energy systems globally.

Quantitative Methods for HIV Researchers

Advisor: Cliburn Chan, Justin Pollara

Duke Center for AIDS Research

I conducted quantitative research to explore the distribution of genotypes/allotypes for IgG and FcR genes in the specific regions and populations where HIV vaccines are being evaluated.
