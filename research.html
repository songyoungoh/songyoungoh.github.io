Research

A selection of past and present research endeavors.

Exploring Query Policies and Uncertainty Visualizations of Active Learning System

Advisor: Danielle Albers Szafir | Mentor: Ian Thomas

VisuaLab at UNC Chapel Hill

I actively contributed to an empirical study that explored the interplay between query policies and visualizations of active learning system certainty in shaping analyst trust and performance in the image classification problems.

Building a Visual Analytic Tool for Individuals Living with Parkinson's Disease

Advisor: Danielle Albers Szafir | Mentor: Jade Kandel

VisuaLab at UNC Chapel Hill

I participated in developing an interactive system to monitor the daily activities of individuals living with Parkinson's disease, integrating augmented reality technology and immersive analytics components.

Tracking Climate Change with Satellites and Artificial Intelligence

Advisor: Kyle Bradbury

Duke Bass Connections Team

I am currently leading team efforts as a graduate mentor to analyze satellite imagery and other remotely sensed data using computer vision techniques to better understand and plan global energy systems.

Constructing Quantitative Methods for HIV Researchers

Advisor: Cliburn Chan, Justin Pollara

Duke Center for AIDS Research

[Poster]

I conducted quantitative research to explore the distribution of genotypes/allotypes for IgG and FcR genes in the specific regions and populations where HIV vaccines are being evaluated.

Detecting Patterns of Drug Diversion in a Controlled Substance

Advisor: Andrea Lane | Client: Department of Anesthesiology at Duke Health

Duke MIDS Capstone Project Team

I am engaged in developing meta-ensemble models, encompassing supervised/unsupervised learning and social network analysis, to compute weighted "Care Flags" based on weak predictors and classifiers.
