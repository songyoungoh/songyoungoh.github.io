Song Young Oh

Second-Year Master's Student in Interdisciplinary Data Science

songyoung.oh {at} duke.edu

I am completing my degree in interdisciplinary data science at Duke University. My research interests lie at the intersection of data visualization and human-computer interaction, exploring how we perceive visual data and translate this perception into actionable insights. I am also interested in examining the symbiotic relationship between humans and artificial intelligence, harnessing their combined potential to decode complex datasets and design interactive visualization systems. My aim is to develop visual analytic tools that seamlessly integrate human expertise and machine capabilities for enhanced transparency and user-friendliness in computational processes.

Please feel free to pronounce my first name as Song . In my free time, I enjoy music and nature walks!
