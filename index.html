I'm pursuing a master's degree in interdisciplinary data science at Duke University, with an expected completion date in May 2024. My research interests lie at the intersection of data visualization and human-computer interaction, exploring how we perceive visual data and translate this perception into actionable insights. I am also interested in examining the symbiotic relationship between humans and artificial intelligence, harnessing their combined potential to decode complex datasets and design interactive visualization systems. My aim is to develop visual analytic tools that seamlessly integrate human expertise and machine capabilities for enhanced transparency and user-friendliness in computational processes.
